sunday morning.

a quiet morning, coffee, sunlight, and a few thoughts I wanted to keep.

Some weeks are remembered by big events. Others stay because of one quiet morning, a cup left on the table, light moving slowly across the room, or a thought that arrived without asking for attention.

“I think I want to remember ordinary days exactly because they are ordinary.”

This is where each weekly note will live. Photos can interrupt the text, captions can sit in the margins, and small interface details can make every post feel like another track in the archive.

The visual language stays connected to the tape player without turning the article itself into a gimmick. Once reading begins, the page becomes calmer and more editorial.
